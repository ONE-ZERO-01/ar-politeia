V1H is a deterministic reanalysis of V1F's retained replicate_metrics.csv. It reads no
random values, initialises no state, and draws no new random numbers, so it consumes no
seeds and holds no seeds.txt.

The 64 seeds below are NOT consumed. They label the V1F runs whose tail-window metrics the
retained table contains, and naming them here keeps the reanalysis's source auditable: this
list must stay equal to V1F-NONFLAT-CALIBRATION-C4/seeds.txt. jobctl requires a seed
declaration at submit time, so these are what were passed there — as provenance, not
consumption. Nothing in V1H re-draws any of them, so no random stream is shared with V1F or
with any other job, and the seed ledger records this job as a consumer of nothing.
